Known unknowns: whether `kinematicsFlags` ever takes values other than `-1` (untaught) and `4` (taught); when PolyScope refreshes a Script node's
`cachedContents`; how PolyScope handles overlapping blend radii on a MoveJ with closely spaced waypoints; what
a physical pendant does when a program's `crcValue` differs from its safety checksum; what the occasional
"Could not find inverse kinematics" log lines in URSim mean (they did not track file content: an empty
program produced them too, and they did not stop a load).

URSim limits: no Robotiq, palletizing or other URCaps; its own stock installation and nominal kinematics
differ from a real robot's, so "loads in URSim" is evidence that the file parses, not that it is right for your
robot.
